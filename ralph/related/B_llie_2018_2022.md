# Deep Low-Light Image Enhancement (LLIE): Canonical Lineage 2018–2022

Slice B of literature scouting for a bachelor's thesis on HVI-CIDNet (Yan et al., CVPR 2025,
arXiv:2502.20272). Covers the deep-learning LLIE lineage from RetinexNet (2018) through the
2022 CVPR/AAAI/BMVC crop that HVI-CIDNet's own comparison tables draw on.

---

### wei2018retinexnet
- Title: Deep Retinex Decomposition for Low-Light Enhancement
- Authors: Chen Wei, Wenjing Wang, Wenhan Yang, et al.
- Venue: BMVC 2018 (Tier 2) — VERIFIED: yes (arXiv 1808.04560; official project page daooshee.github.io/BMVC2018website)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.04560
- Family: Retinex-decomposition
- One-line idea: Decom-Net splits a low-light image into reflectance and structure-aware smooth illumination; Enhance-Net adjusts illumination; introduces the paired LOL dataset.
- Relevance to HVI-CIDNet: Origin of the Retinex decomposition paradigm and the LOL benchmark that HVI-CIDNet trains/evaluates on; the paper HVI-CIDNet's whole lineage traces back to.
- Reported LOL-v1 PSNR if stated: 16.77
```bibtex
@inproceedings{wei2018retinexnet, title={Deep Retinex Decomposition for Low-Light Enhancement}, author={Wei, Chen and Wang, Wenjing and Yang, Wenhan and Liu, Jiaying}, booktitle={British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC)}, year={2018}}
```

### zhang2019kind
- Title: Kindling the Darkness: A Practical Low-light Image Enhancer
- Authors: Yonghua Zhang, Jiawan Zhang, Xiaojie Guo
- Venue: ACM MM 2019 (Tier 2) — VERIFIED: yes (ACM DOI 10.1145/3343031.3350926)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04161
- Family: Retinex-decomposition
- One-line idea: Decomposes into illumination and reflectance with dedicated sub-networks (Decom/Restoration/Adjustment) trained without ground-truth decomposition labels, enabling arbitrary light-level adjustment.
- Relevance to HVI-CIDNet: Canonical Retinex-based two-branch baseline; standard comparison method in LOL-benchmark tables.
- Reported LOL-v1 PSNR if stated: 20.86
```bibtex
@inproceedings{zhang2019kind, title={Kindling the Darkness: A Practical Low-light Image Enhancer}, author={Zhang, Yonghua and Zhang, Jiawan and Guo, Xiaojie}, booktitle={Proceedings of the 27th ACM International Conference on Multimedia (ACM MM)}, pages={1632--1640}, year={2019}}
```

### yang2020drbn
- Title: From Fidelity to Perceptual Quality: A Semi-Supervised Approach for Low-Light Image Enhancement
- Authors: Wenhan Yang, Shiqi Wang, Yuming Fang, et al.
- Venue: CVPR 2020 (Tier 1) — VERIFIED: yes (openaccess.thecvf.com content_CVPR_2020)

- Family: other (semi-supervised band-recursive)
- One-line idea: Deep Recursive Band Network (DRBN) recovers coarse-to-fine linear bands from paired data, then recomposes them for perceptual quality via adversarial learning on unpaired data.
- Relevance to HVI-CIDNet: Standard semi-supervised baseline in LOL-v1 comparison tables; represents the perceptual/adversarial refinement branch of the lineage.

### guo2020zerodce
- Title: Zero-Reference Deep Curve Estimation for Low-Light Image Enhancement
- Authors: Chunle Guo, Chongyi Li, Jichang Guo, et al.
- Venue: CVPR 2020 (Tier 1) — VERIFIED: yes (openaccess.thecvf.com content_CVPR_2020)
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06826
- Family: curve/zero-reference
- One-line idea: Trains a lightweight DCE-Net to estimate pixel-wise high-order enhancement curves using only non-reference losses (spatial consistency, exposure, color constancy, illumination smoothness) — no paired or unpaired training data.
- Relevance to HVI-CIDNet: Canonical zero-reference/curve baseline representing the unsupervised end of the LLIE spectrum contrasted with HVI-CIDNet's supervised paired training.
- Reported LOL-v1 PSNR if stated: n/a (paper reports non-reference metrics and general benchmarks, not a native LOL PSNR table; ~14.86 is a commonly cited figure from later comparison papers, not independently confirmed here)
```bibtex
@inproceedings{guo2020zerodce, title={Zero-Reference Deep Curve Estimation for Low-Light Image Enhancement}, author={Guo, Chunle and Li, Chongyi and Guo, Jichang and Loy, Chen Change and Hou, Junhui and Kwong, Sam and Cong, Runmin}, booktitle={Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)}, pages={1780--1789}, year={2020}}
```

## Datasets

- **LOL-v1 (485 train / 15 test)** — introduced in Wei, Wang, Yang, Liu, "Deep Retinex Decomposition for Low-Light Enhancement," BMVC 2018 (500 low/normal-light pairs total). VERIFIED: yes (arXiv 1808.04560; daooshee.github.io/BMVC2018website).
- **LOL-v2-real** and **LOL-v2-synthetic** — introduced in Yang, Wang, Huang, Wang, Liu, "Sparse Gradient Regularized Deep Retinex Network for Robust Low-Light Image Enhancement," TIP 2021 (real captured pairs + Real2Synthetic synthetic subset). VERIFIED: yes (DOI 10.1109/TIP.2021.3050850).
- **SID (See-in-the-Dark)** — introduced in Chen, Chen, Xu, Koltun, "Learning to See in the Dark," CVPR 2018 (5,094 raw short/long-exposure pairs, Sony + Fuji cameras). VERIFIED: yes (openaccess.thecvf.com content_cvpr_2018).
- **SICE** — introduced in Cai, Gu, Zhang, "Learning a Deep Single Image Contrast Enhancer from Multi-Exposure Images," TIP 2018 (589 multi-exposure sequences, 4,413 images). VERIFIED: yes (DOI 10.1109/TIP.2018.2794218).
- **LOL-Blur** — introduced in Zhou, Li, Loy, "LEDNet: Joint Low-light Enhancement and Deblurring in the Dark," ECCV 2022 (12,000 low-light-and-blur/normal-sharp pairs from 170 train + 30 test videos). VERIFIED: yes (arXiv 2202.03373; ECVA/Springer ECCV 2022 proceedings).
