# Novelty check — M3: chroma-confidence gating in the HV branch

## Verdict: INCREMENTAL
The core idea — derive a confidence/gating signal from intensity (and implicitly noise/saturation) to attenuate or recalibrate unreliable chroma in dark regions of an HVI-CIDNet-family network — is already colonized by at least four 2026 works, most directly **CMIG-Net** (arXiv 2608.01886), which extends CIDNet with a "Conditional Information Map" that region-adaptively recalibrates chrominance *conditioned on intensity*, and **ICDNet** (arXiv 2605.02627), whose "intensity-aware chromaticity gate" (α = lower confidence bound, γ = learnable exponent) explicitly attenuates chromaticity in low-intensity pixels.
WIRNet's Gated-LCA (NTIRE 2026) gates the very Lighten Cross-Attention blocks M3 targets, and TCA-Net's DDSGM/thresholded cross-attention (arXiv 2607.13925) suppresses "chromaticity leakage" via a confidence threshold. None of these were confirmed (from abstracts/available text) to (a) fall back to a **guided-filter/learned-propagation spatially-smoothed chroma prior** anchored on enhanced intensity, or (b) report **hue error/ΔE00 broken out by dark-intensity decile** as the headline metric, or (c) train with an explicit **confidence-weighted chroma loss** matching the gate — so a version of M3 built around that specific triplet could still be defensible, but the "gate the HV branch by an intensity-derived confidence" framing itself is not new.

## The strongest baseline M3 must beat
CIDNet/HVI-CIDNet+ augmented with **CMIG-Net's conditional-information chrominance recalibration** and/or **TCA-Net's confidence-thresholded I↔HV cross-attention + DDSGM residual-intensity chroma-leakage suppression**, evaluated on LOL-v1/LOLv2-Real. These already gate/recalibrate chroma by an intensity-derived signal inside the HVI-CIDNet lineage at competitive PSNR/SSIM; M3 must show its guided-filter fallback and confidence-weighted loss buy something these gating/recalibration mechanisms don't (lower dark-decile hue/ΔE00, fewer visible color-noise artifacts) at matched global PSNR/SSIM.

## A version of M3 that would be clearly novel
Keep the confidence gate but make the low-confidence branch an explicit **guided-filter/learned-propagation blend toward an intensity-anchored smoothed chroma prior** (not just attenuation/recalibration/thresholded attention), pair it with a **confidence-weighted circular-hue loss**, and report **hue error/ΔE00 binned by darkest intensity deciles** plus a color-noise-artifact count — none of the surveyed works confirm this specific combination (mechanism + loss + decile-binned protocol) together.

## Cheapest experiment that decides it
Pull public checkpoints/outputs for CIDNet, CMIG-Net (or TCA-Net) on LOL-v1/LOLv2-Real; on a handful of dark validation images, bin pixels by intensity decile and compute hue error/ΔE00 per bin from their released outputs versus a quick guided-filter-gated CIDNet variant. If CMIG-Net/TCA-Net already close most of the dark-decile gap without the guided-filter fallback, the fallback mechanism isn't earning its keep and M3 collapses to a re-parameterization of existing gating work.
