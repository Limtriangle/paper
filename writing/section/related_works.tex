\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Learned low-light enhancement.} Retinex-inspired decomposition networks
\citep{wei2018retinex}, curve-estimation methods that need no paired data
\citep{guo2020zerodce}, and transformer enhancers \citep{cai2023retinexformer} form the
main families evaluated on paired low-light benchmarks. \mname \citep{yan2025hvi} differs by
changing the color space in which enhancement and supervision happen rather than the
backbone.

\paragraph{Perceptual and structural losses.} SSIM \citep{wang2004ssim}, VGG feature losses
\citep{johnson2016perceptual}, and LPIPS \citep{zhang2018lpips} are standard components and
evaluation metrics for restoration.
