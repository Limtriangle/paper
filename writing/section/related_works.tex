\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Learned low-light enhancement.} Retinex-inspired decomposition networks
\citep{wei2018retinex}, curve-estimation methods that need no paired data
\citep{guo2020zerodce}, and transformer enhancers \citep{cai2023retinexformer} form the
main families evaluated on \dataset. \mname \citep{yan2025hvi} differs by changing the
color space in which enhancement and supervision happen rather than the backbone.

\paragraph{Color spaces for enhancement.} Separating luminance from chroma is a long-standing
idea in enhancement; the \hvi space adds a polar chroma parameterization intended to reduce
color artifacts near black. To our knowledge, the individual contribution of supervising in
that space, as opposed to operating in it, has not been isolated before.

\paragraph{Perceptual and structural losses.} SSIM \citep{wang2004ssim}, VGG feature losses
\citep{johnson2016perceptual}, and LPIPS \citep{zhang2018lpips} are standard components and
evaluation metrics for restoration. Our ablation asks which of them still matter when
duplicated in a second color space.
