\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Retinex-based enhancement.} Retinex theory \citep{land1977retinex} models an
image as the product of illumination and reflectance, and most learned low-light enhancers
descend from it. RetinexNet \citep{wei2018retinex} learns the decomposition end to end and
introduced the \dataset benchmark; KinD \citep{zhang2019kind} enhances illumination and
reflectance along separate branches; URetinex-Net \citep{wu2022uretinex} and RUAS
\citep{liu2021ruas} unroll the decomposition into a network, the latter with architecture
search. Retinexformer \citep{cai2023retinexformer} folds the illumination estimate into a
one-stage transformer and is the published model closest in size to \mname.

\paragraph{Supervised enhancers in other formulations.} LLFlow \citep{wang2022llflow} models
the conditional distribution of normal-light images with a normalizing flow; SNR-Aware
\citep{xu2022snr} routes between local and global processing by a spatial signal-to-noise
estimate; LLFormer \citep{wang2023llformer} targets ultra-high-definition inputs; GSAD
\citep{hou2023gsad} and Diff-Retinex \citep{yi2023diffretinex} use diffusion models. The
channel-attention block inside \mname follows the design of Restormer
\citep{zamir2022restormer}. LEDNet \citep{zhou2022lolblur} treats enhancement and deblurring
jointly and introduced the LOL-Blur dataset.

\paragraph{Unsupervised and zero-reference methods.} Zero-DCE \citep{guo2020zerodce}
estimates per-pixel enhancement curves without paired data; EnlightenGAN
\citep{jiang2021enlightengan} trains adversarially on unpaired images; SCI \citep{ma2022sci}
learns a self-calibrated illumination map with a very small network. \mname is trained with
pairs only and its authors list unsupervised training as open.

\paragraph{Color spaces for enhancement.} Most of the methods above operate on sRGB. LYT-Net
\citep{brateanu2025lytnet} processes luminance and chrominance separately in YUV. \mname
\citep{yan2025hvi} instead maps sRGB into a polar Horizontal/Vertical/Intensity (\hvi)
space in which hue is an angle, saturation a radius, and the radius is shrunk toward the
origin as intensity goes to zero by a learned collapse function; the network enhances
intensity and chroma along two interacting branches and is supervised in both sRGB and
\hvi. An earlier version of the method \citep{yan2024onecolorspace} and a successor for
extreme darkness \citep{yan2025hvicidnetplus} exist; we study the CVPR 2025 version whose
code is public.

\paragraph{Datasets and metrics.} We use the paired \dataset benchmark of
\citet{wei2018retinex}; its successor LOL-v2 \citep{yang2021lolv2}, SID \citep{chen2018sid},
SICE \citep{cai2018sice}, and LOL-Blur \citep{zhou2022lolblur} are the other paired sets on
which \mname was reported. Full-reference quality is measured by PSNR, SSIM
\citep{wang2004ssim}, and LPIPS \citep{zhang2018lpips}; no-reference quality by NIQE
\citep{mittal2013niqe}. The perceptual loss term of \mname is the VGG feature loss of
\citet{johnson2016perceptual}. Color fidelity, when we report it, uses the CIEDE2000
difference \citep{sharma2005ciede2000}.
