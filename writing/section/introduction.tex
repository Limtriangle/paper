\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Low-light image enhancement recovers a well-exposed image from an underexposed capture.
Learned enhancers now dominate the paired benchmarks \citep{wei2018retinex,
cai2023retinexformer, guo2020zerodce}, and a recent line of work argues that the color
space in which the network operates and is supervised matters as much as the architecture.
\mname \citep{yan2025hvi} maps images into a polar Horizontal/Vertical/Intensity (\hvi)
space, enhances intensity and chroma along separate interacting branches, and is trained
with a loss summed over both the RGB output and its \hvi transform.

This paper asks one question about that design. The question, the claims it supports, and
the experiments that decide it are stated in \Cref{sec:method}; the results follow in
\Cref{sec:experiments}.
