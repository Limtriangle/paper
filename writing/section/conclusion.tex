\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under one frozen protocol with five matched seeds (seed SD \phm{0.16}~dB, minimum detectable
step \ph{0.5}~dB), two of \mname's eight design steps contribute: \hvi-space supervision
(\ph{0.6}~dB) and the intensity collapse (\ph{0.4}~dB, in the darkest deciles). The VGG term
on \hvi, loss-side coupling of $k$, learning $k$, and the max intensity are removable or
inconclusive, and the published spread is rescaling, the gate, selection, and seed noise.
