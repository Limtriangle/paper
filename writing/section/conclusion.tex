\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Under one frozen protocol on \dataset, the \hvi-space perceptual term is the only
\hvi-space loss component whose removal moves \mname by more than the seed spread, width
buys \ph{0.6}\,dB for \ph{4}$\times$ the parameters, and the intensity-only prefilter does
not replicate on fresh seeds. Every number here is bound to a result file; the protocol and
scripts are released with the thesis.
