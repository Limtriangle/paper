\section{Results}
\label{sec:experiments}



\paragraph{Reproduction under the frozen protocol.} The \phm{5} L0 runs of the released
recipe reach a raw test PSNR between \phm{22.78} and \phm{23.97}~dB at the final
checkpoint, mean \phm{23.25}~dB with a seed SD of \phm{0.47}~dB, and between \phm{26.52}
and \phm{26.90}~dB after the GT-mean gain, mean \phm{26.65}~dB with a seed SD of
\phm{0.16}~dB (per seed in \Cref{tab:app-gatea}). The raw final-checkpoint mean misses the pre-registered reproduction window,
\ph{23.3}--\ph{24.3}~dB, by $\phm{-0.05}$~dB, so pivot P-A applies, while two other raw reads of the same runs
sit inside the window: the test-split oracle over checkpoints, \phm{23.66}~dB, and the
final checkpoint with the upstream saturation gate, \phm{23.67}~dB. Gated and GT-mean
together, the checkpoints score \phm{27.55}--\phm{28.05}~dB (mean \phm{27.73}~dB; the
gate adds \phm{1.09}~dB), matching the \phm{27.71}~dB the authors report for their
checkpoint. The shortfall coincides with three protocol differences: the final rather than a
test-selected checkpoint, no gate, and \phm{445} rather than 485 training images; selection
and gating are measured here, the training-set reduction is not isolated.
Every absolute number in this paper is therefore \emph{under our protocol}; the contrasts
are unaffected, since every arm shares it. The separate seed-SD criterion passes (\phm{0.47}~dB $\le 0.5$~dB), but the value lies in
the $0.4$--$0.5$ band, so seeds 47--49 were added to A0 and will be added to A2 and A3. Pooled over all
\phm{8} seeds, the released recipe reaches \phm{23.26}~dB raw (SD \phm{0.41}~dB),
$\phm{-0.04}$~dB from the window's lower edge; the gate verdict stays on the
pre-registered five. The learned $k$ ends at a median of \phm{0.85},
and the loss ladder's achieved MDE is \phm{0.34}~dB.

\paragraph{Loss ladder.} \Cref{tab:s1} reports the four nested contrasts. Removing the VGG term from the \hvi-space loss lowers GT-mean PSNR by \phm{0.07}~dB,
within noise: the perceptual term on non-RGB tensors is \phm{removable} (TOST
$p = \phm{0.02}$).
Detaching $k$ from the loss transform raises it: L1$-$L2 is $\phm{-0.16}$~dB, negative in
\phm{5} of 5 seeds;
the Holm interval excludes zero, but the effect is inside $\pm 0.3$~dB, so it is
\phm{removable} under the pre-registered rules (TOST $p = \phm{0.004}$). It changes what $k$ does: coupled through the loss (L0), $k$ converges from its initial
\phm{0.2} to a median of \phm{0.85} over five seeds; detached (L2), it stops at a median of
\phm{0.29}; the released checkpoint carries \phm{1.13}. Removing \hvi-space supervision entirely costs \phm{0.58}~dB, with a Holm
interval of [\phm{0.25}, \phm{0.90}]~dB, a mean more than twice its SD of \phm{0.17}~dB,
the sign in \phm{5} of 5 seeds, and the pre-registered mixed model agreeing in direction
(coefficient \phm{0.58}; \Cref{sec:app-mixed}), so the second loss space \phm{contributes}; it is the one loss step that does. Weighting the \hvi chroma terms by the collapse $\Ck(I_{\mathrm{gt}})$ lowers it by
\phm{0.15}~dB; the interval
includes zero and equivalence is not shown, so it is \phm{inconclusive} (TOST
$p = \phm{0.09}$). The frozen loss used in the representation ladder, L2, therefore differs from the released
recipe by two removable steps.

\paragraph{What the coupling of $k$ does.} In the released code $k$ receives gradient only
through the forward transform, so coupling it into the loss lets the network shrink the
second loss term by collapsing chroma in the target as much as in the prediction; L2 removes
that shortcut. A quality change inside the margin beside a threefold change in final $k$ makes the learned
collapse a property of the objective, hence the detached loss in the representation ladder.

\paragraph{Representation ladder.} \Cref{tab:s2} isolates the four representation steps
under L2. Because $k$ moves from \phm{0.2} to a median of \phm{0.29} in the A0 runs,
above the $0.05$ threshold at which A1 would have been dropped, the learned-versus-fixed
contrast is real, and it is removable: fixing $k$ at the converged value changes nothing the seeds can
resolve. Removing the
collapse altogether (A0$-$A2) costs \ph{0.4}~dB and is the one representation step that
contributes; the per-decile hue-error and CIEDE2000 differences between A0 and A2 satisfy the
pre-registered mechanism rule of \Cref{sec:method}: the gain sits in the three darkest
intensity deciles, not in the five brightest, and it grows under the darkness stress test,
the pattern the collapse mechanism predicts. Replacing polar chroma by linear
opponent chroma at the same intensity (A2$-$A3) changes quality by \ph{0.1}~dB and is
inconclusive, and replacing the max intensity by luma (A3$-$A4) changes raw PSNR by
\ph{0.2}~dB but GT-mean PSNR by \ph{0.0}~dB: the max-versus-luma choice is an exposure
effect, absorbed by one scalar gain.

\paragraph{Seed variance and selection bias.}
\label{sec:selection}
For the released recipe the seed SD of test GT-mean PSNR is \phm{0.16}~dB (\phm{0.47}~dB on
raw PSNR). The post-hoc oracle, which
scores every tenth checkpoint on the test split, exceeds the final checkpoint by
\phm{0.41}~dB of raw PSNR but by only \phm{0.06}~dB of GT-mean PSNR: test-set selection
mostly picks checkpoints whose brightness happens to match, and the GT-mean gain absorbs
that. Its maximum over seeds, the number a paper would report after test-split selection,
is in \Cref{tab:app-s3}, partial (L0, L1, L2) until all loss-arm oracle passes land. The \phm{23.6}--\phm{28.2}~dB spread of reported \dataset values
\citep{yan2025hvi, hvicidnet_issue66} is therefore mostly the GT-mean gain (\phm{3.39}~dB) and the saturation gate
(\phm{1.09}~dB), with selection adding \phm{0.06}~dB; seed noise is a separate spread of
\phm{0.16}~dB around each mean. Oracle numbers never rank arms; they size the practice and justify the final-checkpoint
read-out.



\paragraph{The recipe that follows.} Keep the \hvi space with its collapse and a second
loss space; drop the VGG term on \hvi and the loss-side coupling of $k$; choose the rest by
convenience at the \phm{0.34}~dB resolution of five seeds (details, including LOL-v2-Real transfer, in
\Cref{sec:app-experiments}).
