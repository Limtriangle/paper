\paragraph{Width.} \Cref{tab:width} reports the width sweep. Widening from $W=18$ to
$W=36$ raises validation PSNR by \ph{0.6}\,dB and lowers LPIPS by \ph{0.03}, at a
\ph{4}$\times$ increase in parameters. The gain from 30 to 36 channels is \ph{0.1}\,dB,
within the seed spread we measure at $W=18$ (\ph{0.2}\,dB).

\paragraph{Loss ablation.} Removing the \hvi-space perceptual term costs \ph{0.3}\,dB of
PSNR and \ph{0.02} LPIPS at $W=18$; removing any other single \hvi-space term changes
PSNR by less than \ph{0.1}\,dB. Removing all four \hvi-space terms at once (RGB-only
training) costs \ph{0.4}\,dB. The full table is in \Cref{sec:app-loss}.

\paragraph{Input prefilter.} On the exploratory seed, the intensity-only prefilter (I3)
improves LPIPS and SSIM over the baseline (\Cref{tab:prefilter}). A pre-registered
confirmation on two fresh seeds required LPIPS lower, SSIM higher, and PSNR within
0.10\,dB of the baseline at the selected checkpoint. Seed 43 passes every criterion and
seed 44 fails every criterion, so the screen returns \emph{hold}: the exploratory gain does
not replicate, and we do not adopt the prefilter.

Further per-image analysis and the complete ablation tables are in \Cref{sec:app-experiments}.
